# Privacy policy

**AP Learner's Licence — Practice Test** (the app)
Effective: 21 September 2026 · Version 1.0

## The short version

The app collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs. Nothing you do in the app — answers, scores, bookmarks, settings — is sent anywhere. It all stays on your phone, and deleting the app deletes it.

## What the app stores, and where

The app keeps your practice history on your device only, in its private app storage:

- mock-test attempts: which questions were asked, what you answered, the score and time taken;
- per-question statistics (how often you have seen and answered a question), used to choose weak areas;
- bookmarks;
- settings: appearance (light or dark).

None of this identifies you. It is not encrypted separately, because the app holds nothing sensitive; it is protected by the same app sandbox your phone applies to every app. **Reset progress** in Settings erases all of it, and uninstalling the app does too.

Your phone's own backup service (Google backup on Android, iCloud backup on iOS) may include the app's data as part of your device backup, under your own account and your own settings. The app has no control over, and no access to, those backups.

## Network use

The app works entirely offline and makes no requests of its own. One exception exists, and we would rather explain it than hide it:

- **Update check after a crash.** The app is built with Expo, and can download a fixed version of its own code if a previous launch failed (`expo-updates`, set to check only on error recovery — never on a normal launch). That check contacts Expo's update service. Like any internet request, it necessarily reveals your device's IP address to that service, together with the app's version and platform. The app sends no identifier of yours and nothing about your use of it. Expo's handling of that request is described in Expo's privacy policy at <https://expo.dev/privacy>.

The app declares the Android `INTERNET` permission for that single purpose. It requests **no runtime permissions**: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or notifications.

Links in the app (to the official transport portals, the source question bank and this policy) open in your browser, which is then governed by those sites' own policies.

## Children

The app is for people preparing for a learner's licence, which in India is possible from age 16. It is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no data, it processes no child's personal data.

## Your rights

There is nothing to access, correct, export or delete on our side, because we hold nothing. Everything is on your device, under your control.

## Changes
