# AP Learner's Licence — Practice Test

An **unofficial**, fully offline study aid for the Andhra Pradesh learner's licence (LLR) computer test: a mock exam in the real format, the full published question bank with plain-language explanations, and every road sign.

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Transport Department, Government of Andhra Pradesh, the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways, Parivahan, or Sarathi. Questions are derived from the LLR question bank the AP Transport Department publishes for candidates. Always verify current rules, fees and the test format on the official portal: <https://www.aptransport.org/> and <https://sarathi.parivahan.gov.in/>.
